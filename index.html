Rows (Vertical): Chord Qualities

From top to bottom, the rows change the "flavor" or quality of the root note in that column.

  • Row 1: Major
  • Row 2: Minor (m)
  • Row 3: Dominant 7th (7)
  • Row 4: Major 7th (M7)
  • Row 5: Minor 7th (m7)
  • Row 6: Augmented (aug / +)
  • Row 7: Diminished (dim / o)
